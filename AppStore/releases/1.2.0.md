# MyStat 1.2.0

The Mac dashboard is easier to read, process lists no longer cover other readings, and Keep Awake can now keep your display on.

- **Side process panels:** CPU and memory lists open to the left or right to fit your screen. Hover to preview, click to pin, or use C / M to switch and Escape to close.
- **Process icons:** recognize apps at a glance, including their helper processes. System processes use a fallback symbol.
- **Redesigned dashboard:** larger readings, compact metric cards, labeled percentage axes, subtle chart grids, and light/dark styling.
- **Keep Awake:** a switch, “Active until” status, and ∞ / 15m / 30m / 45m / 1h / 4h / 8h presets. Keep display on is enabled by default; turn it off to allow display sleep while monitoring continues.
- **Automatic expiry:** macOS enforces timed sessions. Switching off or quitting releases sleep protection. Preferences are remembered, but sessions do not resume on launch. Manual sleep and lid closure still take priority.

**GitHub-only macOS release:** universal Apple silicon / Intel, macOS 12+. This build is ad-hoc signed and not Apple-notarized; macOS may block its first launch. It is a manual download and is not advertised in the automatic update feed.

Quit MyStat before replacing the app in Applications. This updates the Mac companion only; the iOS submission is unchanged.

Validation: 30 core tests, dropdown and side-panel placement checks, Keep Awake lifecycle and real macOS assertion/expiry checks, and a universal release build. The download includes a SHA-256 checksum.
