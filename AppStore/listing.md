## Promotional text

Keep an eye on your Mac from your iPhone. A live desk display for CPU, memory, network, battery flow, and local token usage, with a free Mac companion.

## Description

Your Mac’s stats, right beside you.

MyStat turns your iPhone into a live desk display for your Mac. Install the free Mac companion, connect both devices to the same local network, and see what your Mac is doing at a glance.

SEE MORE OF YOUR MAC
• CPU and memory utilization, plus memory used and total.
• Network download and upload throughput.
• Battery percentage, charging or discharging watts, and reported adapter rating where supported.
• Free storage, swap usage, uptime, and macOS thermal state.
• Today’s aggregate Codex input, cached input, and output tokens from readable local logs.

BUILT FOR YOUR DESK
Open Desk Display for large readings and recent sparklines. Use portrait or landscape, dim the interface, and keep the screen awake while the display is active. Live readings update approximately every two seconds while the app is visible.

EXPLORE YOUR HISTORY
Pinch and pan through up to 24 hours of saved CPU, memory, network, and battery-flow history. Choose between Macs on your network. Lost connections recover automatically, with old readings clearly labeled.

WIDGET SNAPSHOTS
Add a Home Screen or StandBy widget for a quick snapshot. iOS controls widget refresh timing; tap Refresh for a new reading, or open Desk Display for continuous updates.

LOCAL BY DESIGN
No account, advertising, subscription, or developer cloud service. Readings stay on your devices and local network. Try the clearly labeled demo before connecting a Mac.

REQUIREMENTS AND NOTES
Requires iOS 17 or later and the free MyStat Mac companion on macOS 12 or later. Both devices must share a trusted local network. Get the companion at https://cydiater.github.io/MyStat/.

Power readings depend on hardware. Battery watts measure net battery flow, not total Mac or wall-socket consumption; adapter watts are its reported rating. Codex counts reflect recorded local sessions, not billing, account quota, or all AI services. MyStat is independent and is not affiliated with OpenAI. Local network sharing uses unencrypted, read-only HTTP.

## App Review notes

MyStat requires the free companion on a Mac sharing the iPhone’s local network. The app has no sign-in or subscription.

To explore without a Mac: on the initial screen, tap Explore Demo. The dashboard and Desk Display explicitly label all sample readings DEMO. The demo is available to every customer, is memory-only, and does not update widgets or overwrite real history. Tap Connect My Mac to exit.

To test actual readings: download the current Mac companion from the marketing/support website, open it on macOS 12+, and allow MyStat’s local network connections. Open the iPhone app on the same trusted network and allow Local Network access. Select the Mac using the computer menu if needed. Tap Open Desk Display and rotate the phone to test both layouts. Connect once in the main app before adding a widget. Widget refresh timing is controlled by iOS.

The Mac shares system readings and aggregate locally recorded Codex token counts. Conversation content and credentials are not transmitted. Battery readings may be unavailable on desktop Macs or unsupported hardware.
