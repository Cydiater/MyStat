Set up MyStat

  1. On your Mac, download the latest free companion, unzip it, move MyStat to Applications, and open it. macOS 12 or later is required. The menu bar shows two tiny graphs; the app has no Dock icon.
  2. Keep your Mac and iPhone on the same trusted local network. Open MyStat on your iPhone and allow Local Network access.
  3. The iPhone discovers your Mac automatically. Use the computer menu to choose a different Mac.
  4. Tap Open Desk Display for continuous readings. Rotate your phone for a landscape layout. The screen stays awake while Desk Display is active.

The updated 1.0 companion and iPhone App Store release are being prepared. Earlier GitHub releases may show fewer readings. Developers can build the latest source using the repository instructions.

No Mac found?

Check that MyStat is running on the Mac and both devices share a network. On iPhone, enable Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network → MyStat. Guest Wi-Fi, network isolation, or a firewall can block discovery. The Mac’s firewall must permit MyStat’s incoming local connections. Tap Retry after correcting the connection.

Readings stopped?

Wake the Mac. Keep Awake in the Mac menu prevents idle system sleep while the screen sleeps; it does not keep a lid-closed MacBook awake. Leave Desk Display visible for continuous updates. Old readings remain labeled offline with their age.

Widgets and StandBy

Connect in the iPhone app once before adding a widget. Widgets display snapshots, and iOS controls their refresh timing. Tap Refresh for a new reading. Tap the rest of the widget to open Desk Display for live updates.

Some values are unavailable

Battery watts and adapter information depend on hardware. Desktop Macs typically have no battery readings. Adapter watts are a reported rating, not measured wall power. Thermal state is macOS’s assessment, not a temperature reading. Codex tokens use readable local session logs; missing logs show “No local usage.” Counts are not account quota or billing.

Explore before connecting

Tap Explore Demo on the connection screen or in the computer menu. Sample readings are labeled DEMO. Connect My Mac leaves the demo and restores your real connection and history.

Get help

Report an Issue

Include your macOS and iOS versions, MyStat version, and the steps that caused the problem. The tracker is public; omit private device names, usage logs, and other sensitive information.
