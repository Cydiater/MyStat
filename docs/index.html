Made for your desk

  • Large CPU and memory readings in portrait or landscape.
  • Live updates approximately every two seconds while Desk Display is open.
  • History charts for CPU, memory, download, upload, and battery flow.
  • No account, subscription, advertising, or cloud service.

What you need

An iPhone with iOS 17 or later, a Mac with macOS 12 or later, and a shared trusted local network. Install the latest MyStat companion on the Mac and allow Local Network access on iPhone.

Battery readings depend on your Mac’s hardware. Battery watts are not total Mac or wall-socket consumption. Widgets refresh when iOS allows. Codex token counts use readable local logs and do not represent billing or account quota. MyStat is independent and is not affiliated with OpenAI.
