Privacy Policy

Effective September 20, 2026.

MyStat processes your readings on your own devices. The app does not send them to the developer or a cloud service.

Information MyStat uses

The Mac companion reads system measurements such as CPU and memory use, network throughput, battery and power information, storage, uptime, and thermal state. Versions with process rankings also read process names, process identifiers (PIDs), CPU time and resident memory to show the five highest readable processes for each metric. Versions with network app rankings also read per-process TCP/UDP byte counters and combine helpers belonging to the same app. They do not read command arguments, process environments, network endpoints, or packet contents. Versions with Mac process icons use executable paths locally to find the owning application’s icon. These paths and icons are not sent to the iPhone or stored in history. MyStat also reads available local Codex session logs to calculate aggregate token counts. It does not send conversation text, file paths, or credentials to the phone.

Your local network

MyStat uses Bonjour to discover Macs and sends aggregate readings, timestamps, the Mac’s name, and the token measurement date and time zone to your iPhone over the local network. Versions with process rankings also send the top-five lists with names, PIDs, CPU percentages, resident memory and their sampling time. Versions with network app rankings additionally share the top-five app names, representative PIDs, download/upload byte rates and sampling time. Executable paths and icons remain on the Mac. The iPhone sends its device name to the Mac so the Mac can show connected devices.

The Mac serves read-only statistics over unencrypted, unauthenticated local HTTP while the companion is running. Other devices with network access to the Mac may be able to read those statistics. Use MyStat on a trusted network; quit the Mac companion to stop sharing. MyStat does not upload readings to the developer.

Storage and deletion

The Mac holds approximately one hour of readings in memory. The iPhone saves up to 24 hours of history and the selected Mac locally. The app and its widget share the selected Mac and latest snapshot in an App Group container. Process rankings are included in the latest snapshot, but are not added to the historical data. Demo readings are held in memory and are not saved to real history or the widget.

Remove the iPhone app and its widgets to remove their local data. MyStat does not modify or delete Codex’s original logs. Device backups are controlled by your Apple settings.

No advertising or tracking

MyStat has no advertising, tracking, third-party analytics SDK, account system, or developer-operated server. Apple handles App Store purchases under Apple’s policies. MyStat does not receive your payment details.

Mac app updates

Versions with the built-in updater use Sparkle to request a signed update feed from GitHub Pages and download releases from GitHub. Automatic update checks are off initially and can be enabled in the Mac menu; installations require your choice. These requests expose ordinary connection information, such as your IP address and update-client headers, to the hosting providers. System profiling is disabled. System readings, process lists, and local Codex usage are not attached to update requests.

Website and support

This website and our public issue tracker are hosted by GitHub and are subject to GitHub’s privacy policy. If you choose to submit a support issue, the information you include is visible on that public tracker. Do not include sensitive information or private usage logs.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, open a support issue. Any policy changes will be published here with an updated effective date.
